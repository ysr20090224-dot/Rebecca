Things I've worked on.

Muyun Plan — Dairy Farmers Research

I led a research project examining the economic challenges faced by small-scale dairy farmers in rural China. Our team studied cost structures, environmental constraints, and income instability through interviews, observation, and existing research.

Economics Research Sustainability

Campus Cat Shelter

After finding newborn kittens on campus, I worked with other students and a teacher to create a long-term shelter. I helped design an automatic water system and used GIS analysis to choose a suitable location for the shelter.

Community GIS Sustainability
